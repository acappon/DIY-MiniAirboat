## Known issues and open items
- **Requirements (2026-11-03, `reports/Engine_Selection_1448.md`):** driver + 150 lb gear or driver + passenger within the plate, planes easily, at least 33 mph. `CAD/analysis/minimal_1448.js` reports pounds over the plate for each load case and sweeps engine weight against power.
- **800R E-TEC figures are unverified:** the 79 lb bare weight has no cited source, and BRP does not publish its power. Weigh the engine with its pipe, and confirm the thrust on the static test. The payload margin is only 2 lb: if the engine or the stand (target 45 lb) comes out heavier, take the difference out of the outfit.
- **Requirement change (2026-11-03): the design case is the driver + 150 lb of gear; the passenger requirement is dropped.** With the full outfit the 1448 can't carry two 200 lb people under the 825 lb plate (48 lb over). Payload is about 352 lb against 350 lb needed: **only about 2 lb of margin**, so weight added as the details are designed needs an offsetting cut.
- **Electrical:** the light bar, searchlight and navigation lights draw about 15 A (about 200 W). Check the 800R's spare charging output and the battery size.
- **Cage-top lights are high** (Z about 90 in): they add a little to the roll and weathervane moments; check when the stability model is updated.
- **Crank PTO side load:** the belt puts about 1,000 lbf on the PTO end. Confirm the PTO taper and the bearing's capacity from the service manual, or add an outboard support bearing (about 5 lb).
- **Fuel:** about 10.8 gal/h at full power (estimate): a 6 gal tank is about 33 minutes flat out, or about 1.1 hours cruising.
- **Stand and shields (2026-11-03):** the stand is trimmed to 45 lb (first load check in `CAD/analysis/stand_800r.js`; joints and tested vibration still open), heat shields are added, and the stand gets cross-straps and two struts to the cage (about 2.5 lb). Itemized exhaust parts come to about 7 lb, up from 5 lb, the UHMW allowance drops from 35 to 32 lb using the modeled strips, and the passenger cushion and grab handle are removed (−4 lb): equipment about **470.5 lb**, payload about **354.5 lb**, about **+4.5 lb of margin** with driver + 150 lb of gear (`reports/Drivetrain_800R_ETEC.md` Section 9a). BOM line 17 is now the 2 x 3 bed tube, line 43 has the cage bolted to the hull and the stand, and new lines 53–56 list the stand tube, cross-straps and cage struts; BOM total about **$13,370**.
- **Hull mounts (2026-11-03, `reports/Drivetrain_800R_ETEC.md` Section 9b):** the first layout clashed with the rear bench and the rib at X 37.75. The stand now stands on four separate shoes instead of two beds, the rail is at Z 14.9, the aft shoes hang between the transom and the bench's aft panel, and the cage legs stand on the corner caps and the bench top. The bench is not cut. Stand 41.5 lb, hull fastening 7.6 lb: equipment about **474.6 lb**, payload about **350.4 lb**, only about **+0.4 lb of margin** with driver + 150 lb of gear. BOM lines 17–23 updated; BOM total about **$13,172**. Rail-to-bench and rail-to-hoop gaps are tight (0.2 and 0.25 in), the rail's load factor is 1.4, and the bench panels and corner caps must be checked on the real hull. Build Guide steps 2.2–2.7 still describe the old parts.
- **Cockpit (2026-11-03, `reports/Drivetrain_800R_ETEC.md` Section 9c):** the driver sits centered on the factory middle bench and the raised seat is removed. Added: a dry box in the bench with the lithium battery and fuse block, a steering stick to port, a throttle pedal with heel rests, a console to starboard clear above two sets of knees, and the fuel tank with a gauge sender on the front deck step. Equipment about **467.6 lb**, payload about **357.4 lb**, about **+7.4 lb of margin**; solo dock trim about 0.94°. Gear must stow between the benches: in the bow it gives −0.3° of trim and 33.25 mph. BOM lines 35, 36, 38, 44 and 45 updated, new lines 57–61; BOM total about **$13,402**.
- Outfit weights, thrust and prices are estimates. The CAD is a provisional layout: engine, pipe and radiator are placeholder envelopes. Use the tab **Minimal 1448 assembly**; Assembly 1 in Main still shows the rev7 parts, with the passenger removed.
- The engine is far above the 1448 plate's 25 hp outboard rating (registration and insurance).
- Running trim is flat (1.1–1.4°) with the prop axis where the model puts it; check a lower or tilted axis.
- Two-stroke engine life: budget a top-end rebuild at the service manual's interval (top-end kit about $1,300).
