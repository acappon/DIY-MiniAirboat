## Known issues and open items
- **Requirements (2026-11-03, `reports/Engine_Selection_1448.md`):** driver + 150 lb gear or driver + passenger within the plate, planes easily, at least 33 mph. `CAD/analysis/minimal_1448.js` reports pounds over the plate for each load case and sweeps engine weight against power.
- **800R E-TEC figures are unverified:** the 79 lb bare weight has no cited source, and BRP does not publish its power. Weigh the engine with its pipe, and confirm the thrust on the static test. The payload margin is only 2 lb: if the engine or the stand (target 45 lb) comes out heavier, take the difference out of the outfit.
- **Requirement change (2026-11-03): the design case is the driver + 150 lb of gear; the passenger requirement is dropped.** With the full outfit the 1448 can't carry two 200 lb people under the 825 lb plate (48 lb over). Payload is about 352 lb against 350 lb needed: **only about 2 lb of margin**, so weight added as the details are designed needs an offsetting cut.
- **Electrical:** the light bar, searchlight and navigation lights draw about 15 A (about 200 W). Check the 800R's spare charging output and the battery size.
- **Cage-top lights are high** (Z about 90 in): they add a little to the roll and weathervane moments; check when the stability model is updated.
- **Crank PTO side load:** the belt puts about 1,000 lbf on the PTO end. Confirm the PTO taper and the bearing's capacity from the service manual, or add an outboard support bearing (about 5 lb).
- **Fuel:** about 10.8 gal/h at full power (estimate): a 6 gal tank is about 33 minutes flat out, or about 1.1 hours cruising.
- Outfit weights, thrust and prices are estimates; nothing is modeled in CAD yet.
- The engine is far above the 1448 plate's 25 hp outboard rating (registration and insurance).
- Running trim is flat (1.1–1.4°) with the prop axis where the model puts it; check a lower or tilted axis.
- Two-stroke engine life: budget a top-end rebuild at the service manual's interval (top-end kit about $1,300).
