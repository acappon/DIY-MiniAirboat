# Questions for suppliers — v1.0.1.2

These can only be answered by the makers. Each is written so it can be sent as it stands. Record the answer in `reports/Drivetrain_800R_ETEC.md` Section 9g.

## Warp Drive (641-903-2535, WarpDriveInc.com)

We plan to run a 3-Blade HP propeller, 66 in, on an airboat:

- Engine: Rotax 800R E-TEC two-stroke twin, about 120 hp at the prop shaft, 160 hp at most.
- Reduction: one-stage 8MGT belt, 2.94:1. Prop about 2,590 rpm at the operating point and 2,690 rpm at the 7,900 rpm engine limit.
- Mount: pusher, on a 1-1/2 in shaft with an SAE #2 flange and a 3/8 in face plate.

The HP manual (091-01-23) gives 3,200 prop rpm and 160 hp, and says the engine list is incomplete. Questions:

1. Is this engine and belt reduction approved for the HP hub? A two-stroke twin with no damper is not on the list.
2. Do those limits apply at 66 in on an airboat?
3. Do you recommend a starting pitch for this rpm and diameter?
4. Which rotation do we order, given that the prop turns the same way as the crank?

## Gates Product Application (gates.com/drivedesign)

Drive: Poly Chain GT Carbon 8MGT-2000-36, 32 and 94 grooves, flanged and dynamically balanced, 29.28 in centers. Driver is a two-cylinder two-stroke at 7,600 rpm, 7,900 rpm at most, 120 hp. Driven load is a three-blade airboat prop. There is no clutch or torsional damper.

1. What is the rated horsepower at 7,600 rpm? The manual's table stops at 5,500 rpm. Our extension gives about 239 hp.
2. Is the belt suitable for this speed, at 6,383 ft/min?
3. What service factor do you recommend for a two-cylinder two-stroke, and does it need a torsional damper or a compliant coupling?
4. Is the formula tension of 457 to 499 lb right for this drive? It works out at about 33 to 36 lb to deflect the span 0.45 in.

## BRP dealer, or the Ski-Doo shop manual for your engine's model year

Quote the engine's year and model. Questions:

1. PTO taper dimensions and clutch bolt torque.
2. Side load limit on the PTO bearing. The belt puts about 1,000 lb static on it.
3. Starter current draw, and the battery rating BRP specifies.
4. Charging output and regulator type. Is the regulator safe with a lithium battery, and with the battery disconnected while running?
5. Oil level sensor: does it close on low oil, and what is the pinout?
6. Oil pump wiring and oil line route.
7. Coolant temperature and exhaust temperature limits.
8. Fuel pump current.
9. Engine-side harness: which ECM inputs must be satisfied to run off the sled?
