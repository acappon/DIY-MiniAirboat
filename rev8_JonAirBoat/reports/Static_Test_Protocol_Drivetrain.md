## STOP signal (agree before starting)

- Voice "STOP" **or** hand slashed across the throat **or** two arms crossed overhead means: **operator pulls the kill switch immediately.**
- Throttle up: thumb up and the step number shown with fingers. Throttle to idle: flat hand moving down.

## Part A: Pre-run inspection and setup (engine OFF, locked out)

Lockout in place: key out, lanyard off, battery negative disconnected. ☐

**A1. Structure**
- ☐ Engine beds: all flange bolts present, torque stripes intact, no cracks at welds.
- ☐ A-frame and brace feet: all 3/8 in bolts present, torqued, torque stripes intact.
- ☐ Cage supports and cage: all bolts tight; screens tight; removable section bolted and locked.
- ☐ Bench leg holes: doubler rings tight; no leg touches the bench edges.
- ☐ Mark reference lines with a paint pen across each mount plate/foot joint (to show any movement later).

**A2. Drivetrain**
- ☐ Engine mounts and isolators seated, not bottomed.
- ☐ Redrive belt tension per redrive maker; tension yoke locked; belt guard in place (if fitted).
- ☐ Prop (if installed): bolts torqued, safety wire or torque stripe intact, blades undamaged.
- ☐ Turn the prop by hand two full turns: no rubbing; measure the smallest tip clearance: ______ in (must be ≥ 2 in).
- ☐ Exhaust: hangers and isolators tight; at least 2 in from fuel lines and wiring.

**A3. Fluids and systems**
- ☐ Oil level OK. ☐ Coolant full and bled. ☐ Fuel about 2 gal, cap tight, vent open.
- ☐ No leaks under the engine, at the radiator, hoses, fuel line and filter.
- ☐ Throttle moves full open to full closed freely and **returns to idle by itself**.
- ☐ Rudders move full left/right with no binding; all pivots pinned or wired.
- ☐ Wiring secured away from heat and moving parts; battery strapped down.

**A4. Restraint and site**
- ☐ Boat strapped to the trailer as for towing.
- ☐ Trailer wheels chocked front and back; tongue on the parked tow vehicle (brake on, keys out) or on a heavy stand.
- ☐ Primary restraint: bow eye → crane scale → rated strap/chain (≥ 2,000 lb) → fixed anchor ahead of the boat. Scale zeroed and peak-hold set.
- ☐ Backup restraint: bow eye → separate rated strap → anchor, with 2–3 in of slack (not through the scale).
- ☐ Danger zones marked: 100 ft behind, 25 ft in front, **no one in the prop-disc plane**.
- ☐ Area within 30 ft swept clean of loose items (FOD). Boat interior clear: no rags, tools or loose gear.
- ☐ Two fire extinguishers in place (operator + fire watch). First-aid kit on site.

**A5. Instruments and crew**
- ☐ Reflective tape on one blade root; tachometer reads it from the side, outside the danger zone.
- ☐ Camera on a tripod, recording.
- ☐ Sound-level meter ready.
- ☐ Everyone in PPE: plugs **and** muffs, safety glasses (face shield for anyone near the cage), close-fitting clothes, hair tied back, closed shoes.
- ☐ Safety briefing done: roles, STOP signal, danger zones, abort criteria (Plan section 4), fire plan, where the first-aid kit is.

## Part B: Engine run without the prop (prop removed or belt off)

*Skip Part B only if the engine has already been run in the boat and nothing in the fuel, cooling or exhaust systems has changed since.*

- ☐ B1. Prop removed **or** redrive belt off; lockout removed; battery reconnected (negative last).
- ☐ B2. Operator seated, lanyard on. Test lead calls "CLEAR"; everyone confirms they are in position.
- ☐ B3. Start the engine. Attempts to start: ____ . Idle RPM: ______ . Oil pressure at idle: ______ .
- ☐ B4. Run 3 minutes at idle. Walk around (outside the cage) checking for leaks, smoke and exhaust leaks.
- ☐ B5. Rev gently to about 2,500 engine RPM three times; throttle returns to idle by itself each time.
- ☐ B6. **Kill switch test:** pull the lanyard at idle. Engine stops in ______ s (must be ≤ 2 s).
- ☐ B7. Restart, run to normal operating temperature, note the thermostat opening (upper hose gets hot): ______ °F.
- ☐ B8. Shut down. Lockout. Recheck for leaks; top up coolant once cool.

## Part C: Prop installed, first run at idle

- ☐ C1. Install the prop per Build Guide task 2.15 (if not already); cage section back on and locked; hand-turn check repeated.
- ☐ C2. Re-sweep the area and boat for loose items.
- ☐ C3. All crew in position, outside the prop-disc plane. Test lead calls "CLEAR PROP"; operator repeats "CLEAR PROP".
- ☐ C4. Start the engine. Run at **idle only for 2 minutes**. Watch the cage, belt and mounts; listen for unusual sounds.
- ☐ C5. Prop RPM at idle: ______ . Scale reading at idle: ______ lbf.
- ☐ C6. Shut down, lockout, wait for the prop to stop completely.
- ☐ C7. Inspect: reference marks unchanged ☐; torque stripes intact ☐; tip clearance ______ in ☐; nothing loose ☐; no leaks ☐.

## Part E: Rudder and kill-switch checks at mid throttle (about 50%)

- ☐ E1. Start, warm up, set about 50% on the test lead's signal.
- ☐ E2. Operator moves the rudders slowly full left, then full right, then center. Record the boat's yaw tendency or restraint-line angle change and any rudder buzz or flutter: ________________________________
- ☐ E3. Throttle-return check: operator lets go of the throttle at 50%; it returns to idle by itself. ☐
- ☐ E4. **Kill switch at 50%:** on the test lead's signal, the operator pulls the lanyard. Engine stops in ______ s. Note the prop spin-down time: ______ s.
- ☐ E5. Lockout. Inspect the rudder hinges, pivots and pushrod; all pins and wires in place.

## Part G: Post-run inspection (engine OFF, cool, locked out)

- ☐ G1. Re-torque the prop bolts to the prop maker's spec (first-run re-torque). Value: ______ .
- ☐ G2. Check every structural bolt with the torque wrench (beds, A-frame and brace feet, cage supports). Any that moved: ____________________
- ☐ G3. Reference paint marks: any displacement at any mount? ☐ No ☐ Yes (where/how much): ____________________
- ☐ G4. Inspect welds, mount plates, bench holes, ribs near the beds, and hull bottom inside and out for cracks or new dents (flashlight and mirror).
- ☐ G5. Cage: tip clearance at 4 points: top ____ in, bottom ____ in, port ____ in, starboard ____ in; no witness marks on the cage or screens.
- ☐ G6. Belt: tension and tracking; no glazing, cracks or debris.
- ☐ G7. Leaks: oil ☐ coolant ☐ fuel ☐ exhaust (soot marks) ☐ — all none.
- ☐ G8. Hull: water-test the bottom bolts if any bed or bracket bolt was disturbed.
- ☐ G9. Restraint hardware inspected; no damage to the bow eye or the anchor.
