## STOP signal (agree before starting)

- Voice "STOP" **or** hand slashed across the throat **or** two arms crossed overhead means: **operator pulls the kill switch immediately.**
- Throttle up: thumb up and the step number shown with fingers. Throttle to idle: flat hand moving down.

## Part A: Pre-run inspection and setup (engine OFF, locked out)

Lockout in place: key out, lanyard off, battery switch off. ☐

**A1. Structure**
- ☐ Shoes: all six bolts per shoe present, torque stripes intact; transom and bench clips tight.
- ☐ Stand: all bolts present, torqued and striped; gussets and cross-straps tight; rails clear of the bench top.
- ☐ Bearing plates: bolts and jack bolts locked; no cracks at any petal edge.
- ☐ Cage pads, legs and struts: all bolts tight; mesh tight; removable section bolted and locked.
- ☐ Grab rail: all four pins in, clips closed, cables tight, turnbuckles wired, sling lacing tied off.
- ☐ Flotation foam: bow wedge and both aft corner blocks strapped; clear of the bilge pump and hose; shield in place above the port block.
- ☐ Mark reference lines with a paint pen across each shoe, post foot and bearing plate joint (to show any movement later).
- ☐ Tap test: strike the top of the stand side to side and read the frequency: ______ Hz. Convert to engine RPM (Hz × 60 × 2.94): ______ RPM. Estimate is 3,150–4,200 RPM. Pass through that band; don't hold in it.

**A2. Drivetrain**
- ☐ Engine mounts and isolators seated, not bottomed.
- ☐ Belt tension: a new belt deflects 0.45 in at mid-span with 33–36 lb (static tension 457–499 lb); jack bolts locked; belt guard in place; pulleys aligned.
- ☐ Prop (if installed): bolts torqued, safety wire or torque stripe intact, blades undamaged.
- ☐ Turn the prop by hand two full turns: no rubbing; measure the smallest tip clearance: ______ in (must be ≥ 2 in).
- ☐ Exhaust: springs and ball joint fitted; heat shields tight; pipe guard fitted; wiring at least 9 in from dry exhaust or behind a thermal barrier; tailpipe points aft, never into the prop.

**A3. Fluids and systems**
- ☐ Oil tank full, level marked, line bled per the service manual. ☐ Coolant full and bled. ☐ Fuel about 2 gal of straight gasoline, cap tight, vent open.
- ☐ No leaks under the engine, at the radiator, hoses, fuel line and filter.
- ☐ Pedal moves the throttle full open to full closed freely and **returns to idle by itself**.
- ☐ Stick moves the rudders full left/right to the stops with no binding; stick forward turns left; all pivots pinned or wired; tie bar clears the hoop at full rudder.
- ☐ Wiring secured away from heat and moving parts; battery strapped down; battery switch tested; low oil light tested.

**A4. Restraint and site**
- ☐ Boat strapped to the trailer as for towing.
- ☐ Trailer wheels chocked front and back; tongue on the parked tow vehicle (brake on, keys out) or on a heavy stand.
- ☐ Primary restraint: bow eye → crane scale → rated strap/chain (≥ 2,500 lb) → fixed anchor ahead of the boat. Scale zeroed and peak-hold set.
- ☐ Backup restraint: bow eye → separate rated strap → anchor, with 2–3 in of slack (not through the scale).
- ☐ Danger zones marked: 100 ft behind, 25 ft in front, **no one in the prop-disc plane**.
- ☐ Area within 30 ft swept clean of loose items (FOD). Boat interior clear: no rags, tools or loose gear.
- ☐ Two fire extinguishers in place (operator + fire watch). First-aid kit on site.

**A5. Instruments and crew**
- ☐ Reflective tape on one blade root; tachometer reads it from the side, outside the danger zone.
- ☐ Camera on a tripod, recording.
- ☐ Sound-level meter ready.
- ☐ Everyone in PPE: plugs **and** muffs, safety glasses (face shield for anyone near the cage), close-fitting clothes, hair tied back, closed shoes.
- ☐ Safety briefing done: roles, STOP signal, danger zones, abort criteria (Plan section 4), fire plan, where the first-aid kit is.

## Part B: Engine run without the prop

*Skip Part B only if the engine has already been run in the boat and nothing in the fuel, oil, cooling or exhaust systems has changed since.*

**Caution:** without the prop the engine has almost no load, and a two-stroke can overspeed quickly. Stay under 2,500 RPM. There is also no air through the radiator, so stop if the coolant climbs quickly.

- ☐ B1. Prop removed, belt on; lockout removed; battery switch on.
- ☐ B2. Operator seated, lanyard on. Test lead calls "CLEAR"; everyone confirms they are in position.
- ☐ B3. Start the engine. Crank for no more than 10 s, then wait, to protect the solenoid. Attempts to start: ____ . Idle RPM: ______ . Low oil light off: ☐ .
- ☐ B4. Run 3 minutes at idle. Walk around (outside the cage) checking for leaks, smoke and exhaust leaks.
- ☐ B5. Rev gently to about 2,500 engine RPM three times; throttle returns to idle by itself each time.
- ☐ B6. **Kill switch test:** pull the lanyard at idle. Engine stops in ______ s (must be ≤ 2 s).
- ☐ B7. Restart and run up to thermostat opening if the coolant allows (radiator inlet gets hot): ______ °F. Check for air in the coolant.
- ☐ B8. Shut down. Lockout. Recheck for leaks, including the oil line; top up coolant once cool.

## Part C: Prop installed, first run at idle

- ☐ C1. Install the prop per Build Guide task 2.15 (if not already); cage section back on and locked; hand-turn check repeated.
- ☐ C2. Re-sweep the area and boat for loose items.
- ☐ C3. All crew in position, outside the prop-disc plane. Test lead calls "CLEAR PROP"; operator repeats "CLEAR PROP".
- ☐ C4. Start the engine. Run at **idle only for 2 minutes**. Watch the cage, belt and mounts; listen for unusual sounds.
- ☐ C5. Prop RPM at idle: ______ . Scale reading at idle: ______ lbf.
- ☐ C6. Shut down, lockout, wait for the prop to stop completely.
- ☐ C7. Inspect: reference marks unchanged ☐; torque stripes intact ☐; tip clearance ______ in ☐; nothing loose ☐; no leaks ☐.

## Part D: Stepped throttle runs (shut down and inspect after EACH step)

Hold each throttle setting for **30 seconds** after the RPM settles, then record. Return to idle for 30 s, shut down, lockout, inspect, then continue to the next step. **Never exceed 7,900 engine RPM or 3,200 prop RPM.** **Hold 100% for no more than 1 minute, including the settling time.** If a step lands in the tap-test RPM band, move the throttle until it clears.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 25% | | | | | | | | | |

| D3 | 75% | | | | | | | | | |
| D4 | 100% (7,400–7,500 RPM target) | | | | | | | | | |

Inspection after each step (lockout first): reference marks unchanged · torque stripes intact · tip clearance ≥ 2 in · no leaks · nothing loose · belt tracking OK · no belt flutter on the video.

**Blade pitch:** Warp Drive recommends static RPM 400–500 under the engine maximum, so aim for 7,400–7,500. If D4 is high, add pitch; if low, take it off. Lock out, adjust in 1° steps within the 6–18° range, track the blades, and repeat D3 and D4. Pitch setting: ______ .

## Part E: Rudder and kill-switch checks at mid throttle (about 50%)

- ☐ E1. Start, warm up, set about 50% on the test lead's signal.
- ☐ E2. Operator moves the stick slowly full left, then full right, then center. Record the boat's yaw tendency or restraint-line angle change and any rudder buzz or flutter: ________________________________
- ☐ E3. Throttle-return check: operator takes their foot off the pedal at 50%; it returns to idle by itself. ☐
- ☐ E4. **Kill switch at 50%:** on the test lead's signal, the operator pulls the lanyard. Engine stops in ______ s. Note the prop spin-down time: ______ s.
- ☐ E5. Lockout. Inspect the rudder hinges, pivots, tie bar and cable clamp; all pins and wires in place.

## Part G: Post-run inspection (engine OFF, cool, locked out)

- ☐ G1. Re-torque the prop bolts to the prop maker's spec (first-run re-torque). Value: ______ .
- ☐ G2. Check every structural bolt with the torque wrench (shoes, clips, stand, bearing plates, cage pads, struts, grab rail mounts). Any that moved: ____________________
- ☐ G3. Reference paint marks: any displacement at any mount? ☐ No ☐ Yes (where/how much): ____________________
- ☐ G4. Inspect the gussets, shoes, clips, transom and bench panels at the clips, ribs at the forward shoes, every petal edge on both bearing plates, and the hull bottom inside and out for cracks or new dents (flashlight and mirror).
- ☐ G5. Cage: tip clearance at 4 points: top ____ in, bottom ____ in, port ____ in, starboard ____ in; no witness marks on the cage or mesh.
- ☐ G6. Belt: tension and tracking; no glazing, cracks or debris. Read the spark plugs.
- ☐ G7. Leaks: injection oil ☐ coolant ☐ fuel ☐ exhaust (soot marks) ☐ — all none.
- ☐ G8. Hull: water-test the bottom bolts if any shoe bolt was disturbed. Weigh or estimate the final boat weight.
- ☐ G9. Restraint hardware inspected; no damage to the bow eye or the anchor.
