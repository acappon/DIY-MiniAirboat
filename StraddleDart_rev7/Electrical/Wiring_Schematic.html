Airboat Wiring Schematic

Every circuit from the Group 31 battery to its load, with wire gauge, insulation colour, fuse and route. The same table names the parts in the Fusion model, so the CAD looms carry exactly these wires.

  • FUSES18 AWG is fused 10 A at most; a 20 A fuse only on 12 AWG.
  • RETURNSLights and the 12 V outlet run red/black pairs end to end. No chassis-ground returns.
  • MAIN SWITCHEverything is after the main switch except the automatic bilge pump (ABYC H-22).
  • OUTSIDE THE BAYTinned marine wire, split loom, sealed Deutsch DT/DTP connectors.

Notes

  1. Turn the boat on and off with the key. Use the main switch to change the battery or in an emergency. Don't open it with the engine running: the alternator charges through the 1/0 cable and would lose its load.
  2. The kill-switch lanyard opens the key IGN line to the EFI main relay coil, so pulling it stops ignition, injectors and fuel pump.
  3. The key carries only relay-coil and gauge current (under 2 A), so its 18 AWG on 10 A is protected. Hub fuse 6 is a spare: the gauges take their power from key IGN. The starter solenoid and EFI loads are switched by relays fed on 12 AWG.
  4. The bilge pump runs from its own 10 A fuse at the battery, so the float switch still works with the main switch off.
  5. The portable tank needs an electric fuel sender (US standard 240-33 ohm). Its DT2 connector lets the tank come out.
  6. The light bar and searchlight dimmers take a 3-wire remote input. Check the chosen rotary switch matches the dimmer's input type.
